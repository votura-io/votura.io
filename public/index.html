Cryptography, from proof to production.

Votura is a cryptography research and engineering company. We help teams design, review and build the cryptography their systems depend on, including the move to post-quantum cryptography.

Email us What we do

What we do

  • Research and design

    New constructions and protocols for problems that off-the-shelf cryptography does not solve, each with a clear security argument.

  • Consulting and review

    Independent advice on architecture, protocol choices and threat models, and careful review of designs and code before they ship.

  • Implementation

    Well-tested implementations of cryptographic primitives and protocols, built to be correct, efficient and maintainable.

  • Post-quantum transition

    Moving systems to post-quantum cryptography: finding what is exposed, choosing among the new standards, and rolling out changes step by step.
